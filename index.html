Connect a product to Checkout
Each product needs a Stripe Price ID added to the private stripe_products table. Use the product key shown in your catalog and your workspace ID below.
  1. Create a one-time Price in Stripe Dashboard.
  2. Insert its Price ID into stripe_products with your owner ID, workspace ID, and matching product key.
  3. Deploy the authenticated Checkout function and signed webhook, then test with Stripe test mode.
Checkout buttons only work after these external setup steps. The app does not create Stripe prices or grant entitlements automatically.
